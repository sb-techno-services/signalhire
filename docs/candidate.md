# Candidate workflow

1. Create a candidate account or sign in with the seeded candidate.
2. Search vacancies using the keyword, location, employment type and work arrangement fields. Keyword matches titles, descriptions and company names. Location also matches work arrangement.
3. Open a vacancy and upload a PDF or UTF-8 text CV. Select **Submit application** after reviewing the filename. Each submission keeps its own CV copy.
4. Open **Applications** to track status. **Messages and status history** opens the private thread and lists status transitions.
5. Use **My profile** to edit your name, location and summary. To share your profile in the recruiter directory, open the discovery settings, add skills, and opt in.

**Save vacancy** adds the vacancy to your private list. A saved vacancy still appears there after it closes, with a Closed badge. **Alerts** watches for new publications that match your saved criteria.
