# Recruiter workflow

Set up your company from **Company profile**. The website and benefits fields are optional and appear on your vacancy pages.

In **Recruiter workspace**, create a vacancy draft. Add the location and choose the employment type and work arrangement. Enter the annual salary in pounds for a permanent role or the day rate for a contract. Save and publish. A draft is private to its owning recruiter, and a closed vacancy leaves public search and the JSON feed.

Select **Applicants** to review the received applications. Download the CV submitted with an application, change its status, or open the message thread. Existing applicants can still access their thread and status after the advert closes.

The **Candidate directory** shows professional details for candidates who opted in. Contact information and CVs are available through received applications according to the application's access rules.
